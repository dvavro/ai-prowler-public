How It Works

Pick a tab. Get to work.

The Remote PWA is a Progressive Web App served directly from your AI-Prowler instance. No middleman, no cloud — your data stays on your PC.

Upload & Download files between your phone and PC

The Files tab gives your phone two-way access to your AI-Prowler knowledge base. No cables, no cloud storage account needed.

  • Tap Upload next to any writable folder to send a photo or document from your phone to your PC — it's indexed automatically
  • Tap Download to browse a folder and download any file to your device, with full subfolder navigation and a ← Back button
  • Files download via your browser's native save dialog — the PWA stays open while the file saves
  • Single tracked files (like a contract PDF) get a direct ⬇ Get button — one tap to download

Search your entire library from your phone

The Search tab runs the same semantic search as Claude Desktop — across every document, contract, email, and spreadsheet you've indexed.

  • Type any question or keyword — results appear ranked by relevance
  • Each result shows the source file name and page number
  • Useful for quick lookups in the field when you can't open a computer
  • Searches your full ChromaDB index — the same one Claude uses

Install to your home screen — one tap away

The Remote Control PWA installs like a native app on iOS and Android with no App Store required.

  • iPhone / iPad: Open in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add
  • Android: Open in Chrome → tap ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen → Install
  • Appears on your home screen with the AI-Prowler icon
  • Opens full-screen without browser chrome — feels like a native app
  • Works on any phone or tablet — iOS, Android, and desktop browsers
