Schedule & Calendar
See your own shifts plus the company calendar for holidays, events, and closures.
🏖
PTO & Time Off
Check balances, request time off, and see when a request is approved.

Getting Started

Up and running in minutes.

1

Turn It On

The owner switches on the HR apps in the server’s Settings → MCP Tool Configuration.

2

Add Employees

Add each employee in HR Admin and give them an AI-Prowler account.

3

Scan the QR Code

Employees scan the HR Portal QR code from the server’s Settings tab.

4

Add to Home Screen

Save it to the home screen — it opens full-screen like a native app.

5

Sign In

They sign in with their AI-Prowler account and see only their own information.

6

Use It Daily

Clock in, check the schedule, request time off, and finish trainings.
