HR Admin App

Hire to retire, in one app.

Everything a small business owner or manager needs to run HR — without a separate HR system or a monthly per-employee fee on top.

👥
Employee Records
One place for every employee — personal details, job title, pay, location, documents, and a printable employee file.
🧲
Recruiting & Offers
Track candidates through hiring and generate offer letters with your company name and terms filled in.
📋
Guided Onboarding
Step-by-step onboarding for each new hire, with I-9 and W-4 tracking so nothing is missed before day one.
⏱
Time & Attendance
See who is on the clock right now, review the clock-in/out log with work locations, and track attendance.
🏖
Time Off
Employees request PTO from the portal; you approve or decline from your phone and balances update automatically.
💵
Payroll & Benefits
Keep payroll records and benefits enrollment for each employee in one place, ready for your payroll provider.
⭐
Performance & Training
Run performance reviews and keep a training portfolio for each employee, including certifications and expirations.
⚖
State-Aware Compliance
Built-in federal and state HR rules flag the deadlines and notices that apply where your people work.
🚨
Incident Reports
Employees file incident reports from the portal; you review, follow up, and keep a record.
🗓
Schedules & Tasks
Build team schedules and assign HR tasks with due dates, so the next step is always clear.
🚪
Offboarding
Termination starts a full offboarding flow — final pay receipt, separation and COBRA notices — and ends portal access.
💬
Messages & Reports
Message employees directly and pull HR reports on headcount, hours, time off, and more.
