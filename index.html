How It Works

Set up in minutes. Research in seconds.

AI-Prowler sits between your files and Claude, Muse, and Grok AI. It handles the indexing, embedding, and retrieval — your AI handles the thinking.

01

Index your documents

Point AI-Prowler at any folder on your PC. It extracts text from PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, emails, images (via OCR), and 65+ other formats, then builds a private ChromaDB vector database on your machine.

02

Ask Claude, Muse, or Grok anything

Open Claude Desktop, or connect Claude.ai, Muse, or Grok on the web or mobile. Your AI automatically discovers AI-Prowler’s 100+ tools via MCP and uses them to search, expand, and reason over your documents — going as deep as needed to answer your question.

03

Access from any device

Subscribe for mobile access and your knowledge base travels with you. One activation code sets up a secure Cloudflare tunnel — use Claude.ai, Muse, or Grok from your phone, tablet, or any browser to reach your documents.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a Claude subscription to use AI-Prowler?
No paid Claude plan is required. A free Claude account works for both local use (Claude Desktop via stdio MCP) and mobile access (Claude.ai via HTTPS custom MCP connector). Anthropic now supports custom MCP connections over HTTPS on the free tier, so you no longer need Claude Pro to use AI-Prowler Mobile. Claude Pro is still a great option if you want higher usage limits, but it is not a requirement. Muse and Grok can also connect to AI-Prowler from their web and mobile apps with a mobile subscription.
Does my data leave my computer?
Your documents, the vector database, and all indexed content stay on your PC. The only data that leaves is the text of retrieved document chunks when your AI (Claude, Muse, or Grok) processes them — the same as any conversation with that AI. AI-Prowler never uploads your files to any server.
How does mobile access work technically?
When you subscribe, we provision a Cloudflare tunnel to your PC and send you an activation code by email. Enter the code in AI-Prowler Settings → Remote Access and click Configure — everything sets up automatically. Your PC then has a permanent URL that Claude.ai, Muse, and Grok can reach securely from any device.
What is the difference between Personal and Business?
Personal gives one person mobile access to their own knowledge base on one PC. Business adds server mode — a shared team knowledge base on a company machine — plus a personal AI-Prowler install for each team member. Each Business seat gets both: their own personal knowledge base and access to the shared company one.
