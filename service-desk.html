AI Agents

Two AI agents work the desk for you.

The Service Desk agent sorts new tickets, drafts first replies at any hour, follows up when a customer is waiting and suggests fixes from tickets you've solved before. The Booking agent checks each request, offers open times, confirms bookings, and offers a new time when the weather turns bad.

1

Request comes in

From your support page, email or a text — any time of day.

2

Sorted

The agent tags it, sets the type and puts it in the right queue.

3

Answered

A first reply is drafted — sent on its own, or waiting for your OK.

4

Booked

Open times are offered and the visit is confirmed within your limits.

5

Followed up

If a customer is left waiting, the agent nudges the team or the customer.

6

Learned

It learns from how you close tickets, so the next reply is better.

You program every step.

⚫ Off

The step doesn't run. You handle it yourself.

● Suggest

The agent prepares it and waits for you to approve, edit or reject.

● Auto

The agent does it on its own, inside the limits you set.

◯ Practice mode

See what it would do without it touching anything. Rate each result and switch on when you're ready.

The agent always says it's a virtual assistant, never takes payments, and anything its rules refuse lands in your approval list with the reason. AI agents run on your own Claude (Anthropic) or Grok (xAI) API key — usage is billed by that AI company, and the agents stop when your monthly budget is reached. Without a key, the Service Desk works just as well by hand.
