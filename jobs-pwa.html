Jobs PWA

Built for the field.

Everything a field crew member needs, optimised for a phone screen in any weather.

📋
Today's Schedule
See today's jobs sorted by time, with address, job type, quote amount, and status. Tap any job to open the detail view.
⏱
Clock In / Clock Out
One tap to start and stop the timer on a job. Time is logged automatically to the Jobs_Schedule spreadsheet — no manual entry.
📷
Job Photo Upload
Capture before and after photos from the job detail view. Photos are uploaded to your knowledge base and indexed automatically.
🧾
Create Invoice
Pre-filled invoice form with live tax calculation. Submit to create the record, then email or text it to the customer in one tap.
📍
Navigate to Job
One tap opens the job address in Google Maps or Apple Maps — no copying and pasting.
🔒
Your Jobs Only
In Business server mode, each crew member only sees jobs assigned to their name — not the full team's schedule.
📧
Email & Text Invoices
Email Invoice sends a formatted HTML invoice. Text Invoice sends an SMS with the total and payment link — directly from the app.
📲
Add to Home Screen
Installs like a native app on iPhone and Android — no App Store needed. One-tap access from your home screen.

3

5

Clock Out

6

Invoice & Send

Create the invoice and email or text it to the customer — all from the job screen.
